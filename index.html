Debriefing

This study examines two effects: the misinformation effect, in which inaccurate information presented after an event can influence later memory reports, and confirmation-related feedback effects, in which affirming feedback may change confidence in one's answers.

Participants were randomly assigned to read either an accurate or misleading social media-style post and to receive either strongly confirming or neutral feedback. Feedback was assigned independently of answer accuracy and was not an evaluation of your actual performance. The post may have included an incorrect detail about the road sign and/or which vehicle approached from each side.

The two traffic clips show the same staged collision scenario with different road signs (STOP or YIELD). The clip and experimental conditions were assigned automatically. No identifying information is requested by this page.

If you have questions about the class project, please contact your instructor or the student researcher.
